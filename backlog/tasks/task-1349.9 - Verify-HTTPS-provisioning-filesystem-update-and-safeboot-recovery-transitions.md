## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The separate safeboot image has little flash headroom and currently serves HTTP. The prototype must remain recoverable and explicitly disclose the security boundary across updates.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Certificate/key survival or required re-provisioning is evidenced across reboot, settings reset, filesystem replacement and failed provisioning.
- [ ] #2 Main firmware filesystem upload, safeboot handover and return to the app are tested with documented endpoint/protocol transitions and admin protection.
- [ ] #3 HTTPS and HTTP-only safeboot alternatives have measured sizes and an explicit decision; inability to fit TLS is recorded without silently changing partition layout.
- [ ] #4 Recovery instructions and evidence identify any unencrypted maintenance phase; no end-to-end encrypted-update claim is made for HTTP recovery.
<!-- AC:END -->
