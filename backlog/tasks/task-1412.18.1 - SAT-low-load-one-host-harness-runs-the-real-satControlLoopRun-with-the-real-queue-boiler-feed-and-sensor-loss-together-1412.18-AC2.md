## Implementation Plan

<!-- SECTION:PLAN:BEGIN -->
Approach (chosen): compile the SAT translation unit WHOLE on the host, not cut-out blocks. satControlLoopRun() (SATcontrol.ino:7514-8125, 611 lines, 148 distinct callees) is too wide to cut. Files compiled as they are: SATcontrol.ino, SATcycles.ino, SATpid.ino, SATtypes.h, SATboilerFeed.h, SATflowAvg.h, SATchEnable.h, SATcurveTune.h, SATpm25Poll.h, plus the real PIC command queue of OTGW-Core.ino (addCommandToQueue, handleCommandQueue, checkCommandResponse, the processOT() feed hooks) cut the way tests/test_sat_command_delivery.py and test_sat_no_return_startup.py already cut them (reuse their builders). Everything outside SAT is a stub in one header tests/sat_loop_stubs.h: Arduino (millis from a stub clock), settings/state structs (real SATtypes.h types where they exist), MQTT publish (records only), weather (fixed outside temperature, never stale unless a scenario says so), BLE (no sensor), LittleFS (in-memory, writes recorded), WebSocket ring (drained to nothing). Each stub carries a one-line comment: what it does not model. SATble.ino, SATweather.ino, SATpressure.ino, SATmqttPublish.cpp are NOT compiled (stubbed at their call sites); named as a limit. Boiler: the frame model of test_sat_no_return_startup (thermostat MsgID 25/28 polls, PM=25 answer delay), extended by a first-order water model only where a scenario needs flame/flow coupling; the PIC answers commands by feeding an echo into checkCommandResponse() after a configurable delay or not at all. Driver: a 10 ms stub-clock loop calling processOT() for due frames, handleCommandQueue(), satControlLoop(). Outputs: an event log (CS/MM/CH on the wire, enqueue results, PWM phase, control mode, flow sample ages) that the tests assert on. Scenarios = ACs 1-5; each AC gets a mutant (text edit of the real source, OTGW_SAT_INO-style) that turns that scenario red: 1 PWM ON never ends (phase timer edit), 2 PWM ignores a stale flow, 3 no-return flood disabled, 4 a refused CS cached as delivered (commit-on-success removed), 5 hand-back sent every tick. Risk: the stub surface; measured by the first compile, reported in the notes. If the whole-TU route needs more than ~60 stubs, fall back to cutting satControlLoopRun() plus its direct callee blocks and say so. Not touched: firmware source (test-only task, no bump). Cross-line: no (2.x only). Implementation by a sonnet agent in this worktree; main session reviews raw output.
