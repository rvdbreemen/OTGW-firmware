## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
1.x line (otgw-1.x.x), same defect as the one fixed on 2.x under TASK-1804 (lane-1804, d3, 2026-11-15).

The PIC prints "Error 01" as its own line (PrintError; ErrorStr "Error ", other-projects/otgw-6.6/gateway.asm). On origin/otgw-1.x.x the serial reader cuts every PIC line at CR or LF before the line is handled (OTGW-Core.ino:4916, `if (outByte == '\r' || outByte == '\n') {`), but the error matcher looks for "\r\nError 01" (OTGW-Core.ino:4684, `strstr_P(buf, PSTR("\r\nError 01"))`). That pattern can never match a line without CR/LF, so the error01 counter, its MQTT topic "Error 01" and the web UI event never fire; "Error 02".."Error 04" match without CR/LF and do count. On 2.x the same pattern came from 25663ea01 (2026-02-06); check the 1.x history for its origin. Line numbers are from origin/otgw-1.x.x at the time of filing; locate by content.

Fix (expected one line): match "Error 01" without the CR/LF, as the other three.

Not to build now: 1.x work goes through its own master plan (coordinator, 2026-11-15). Test approach from 2.x: tests/test_core_error_lines.py on the 2.x line (a source test on the real matcher literals: each strstr_P(buf, PSTR("...Error 0N")) pattern must be found in the line "Error 0N" as the reader delivers it, plus a check that the reader cuts at CR/LF); red on the current code, green on the fix.
