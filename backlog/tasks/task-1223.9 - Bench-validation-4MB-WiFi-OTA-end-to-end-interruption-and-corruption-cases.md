## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
2026-10-15 final layout, both board types.
PIC board (Classic-S3, .tmp/bench-migrate/ota.log):
- migrate, handover in 5 s;
- crash-image rollback, and the bad image is not rerun after a reboot;
- a truncated upload is refused;
- 2 MB OTA in 14 s, health gate holds after a hard reset;
- interrupted upload plus RTS reset lands in safeboot, recovered over WiFi;
- auth parity;
- filesystem OTA in 5 s.
OT-Direct board (OTGW32, .tmp/bench-migrate-otgw32/ota.log):
- migrate with --bin combo, settings byte-identical; the API diff shows only the 8 removed ui_* keys (the backup came from alpha.422);
- handover in 6 s;
- crash-image rollback, and the reboot stays in safeboot;
- a truncated upload is refused;
- OTA in 10.5 s, health gate holds after a hard reset;
- interrupted upload plus reset lands in safeboot (app_valid false), recovered in 10 s.
Note: the first OTGW32 series was invalid, because my wait loop took the main firmware's 404 text for safeboot. It was rerun with a JSON check; the log keeps both.
