## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
2026-10-15 implemented:
- POST /safeboot (checkHttpAuth) erases the whole otadata partition, then a deferred reboot follows.
- hasSafeboot() checks the factory app descriptor.
- safebootSyncNvs() runs from read/writeSettings and writes the hostname and password to the otgw-sb namespace only on change.
- verifyRollbackLater() returns true only when a factory partition exists.
- Health gate: safebootMarkAppValid() 30 s after boot, and in doRestart().
- device/info reports safeboot_available, and the /update page shows a 'Restart into the firmware updater' button when it is true.
Bench on the OLD layout (alpha.422+122cb14): safeboot_available false; POST /safeboot answers 409 and does not reboot; /update keeps the USB-only note; 10 min without a reboot loop. evaluate --quick 100%, build green.
AC1 (end-to-end update on the new layout) waits for the layout switch and the TASK-1223.9 bench run.
