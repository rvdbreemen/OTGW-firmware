## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Two related defects on the 1.x line, both absent or already repaired on 2.0.0. checkHttpAuth() short-circuits on OPTIONS, so a CORS preflight reaches every HTTP_ANY FSexplorer route unauthenticated. Separately, collectHeaders() registers only If-None-Match, so ESP8266WebServer discards Origin and Referer and the same-origin CSRF check can never fail, which makes a cross-origin form post to a state-changing route succeed against a browser that holds cached Basic credentials. Found by the FSexplorer audit of 2026-09-01, findings 3 and 4 of 18.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
