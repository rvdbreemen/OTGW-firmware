## Errata

1. **(WRONG, row 42) Section 5 rule (a).** "`SATcontrol.ino:6421-6446` already returns early" does not deliver "no SAT request during DHW". On PIC boards `satPicCSKeepalive()` (:597-606) re-sends the last CS every 30 s during a draw, with no DHW gate. Every-pass senders (`SW=`, `SH=`, `MI=500`) also run outside the tick. Reword: the control tick sends nothing, but the keepalive does, and the PWM MM=0 override stays on the bus (row 38). The doc should not say the current code already meets (a).
2. **(row 38) DHW priority row.** Add the known, verified difference (sat-comparison.md :102, :478, :950). thermo-nova keeps writing during DHW and raises MM to the maximum. The firmware sends no MM, so a PWM `MM=0` stays on the boiler for the whole draw. Entry 21's Task #85 AC "DHW active: MM=max_modulation" agrees with thermo-nova. The "decided" status covers the clock pause and the guard only. This affects the AC3 claim that SAT does not override DHW priority, and it deserves its own question or task.
3. **(rows 1, 9, 10, 32) Off-by-one firmware lines in `satComputeMM`.** `(void)heatPump;` is :470, the PWM branch :471, outside PWM :472. Fix s0 (`:471` → `:470`), gas P5 (`:472` → `:471`, `:473` → `:472`) and the s3.3 entry-21 gas cell (`:472-473` → `:471-472`).
4. **(row 7) Immergas thermo-nova lines.** `opentherm.py:215-217` → `:216-218`; the TP= send is at :218. The value difference is confirmed. Also name the timing difference: thermo-nova sends TP= from the MM setter, the firmware on every control tick with the CS value.
5. **(row 6) Manufacturer table.** Cite `SATcontrol.ino:208-228`, not `:215`. The firmware flags `NO_REL_MOD` on Geminox and Nefit as well as Ideal/Intergas, and `MI_500_BOOT` on Nefit.
6. **(row 16) thermo-nova mapper.** `pwm.py:53-61` are the thresholds. The duty mapper is `_calculate_duty_cycle`, `pwm.py:240-295`, and range 4 (`off_time = _on_time_lower_threshold`) is at :285-287.
7. **(row 53) TC= and entry 14.** Cite entries 14 **and 16**: entry 16 withdrew entry 14's MsgID 9 re-push. Also list the other TC= senders: boot decision :3549-3550 and the tick :6779-6780.
8. **(rows 45, 47) Hand-back paths.** Add the MQTT-fallback lift (:6170) and flashing (:6314) as satDisable callers. Note that the only safety trip (:6790) fires while the command interface is unavailable, so its hand-back may not be delivered. Note that the boot hand-back also pushes `TC=` (:3549-3550).
9. **(row 36)** `:408` belongs to `satEffectiveMaxSetpoint()`, not `satGetMaxSetpoint()`.
10. **(row 20)** OT protocol line 1372 covers ID25 only. ID28 is non-mandatory because it is absent from the 5.2 table.
11. **(row 39)** Defrost has no backend, but the v2 onboarding heat-pump card (`data/v2.js`) advertises "defrost". Either a UI wording task or a note under P10.
12. **(row 43)** Add `PM=25` (:1578) to the list of commands SAT sends. It is a read request, not a persistent write.
13. **(row 11)** The AFS path is `RvdB/OTGW-other-projects/OT-Spec/...`, outside this repo. Say so.
14. **(s11 of the doc)** The entry-81 thermo-nova citations (`pwm.py:156`, `entry_data.py:220`, `const.py:167`) and `heating_control.py:268-274` were opened in this second read and are as recorded. They can move from "not re-opened" to "opened (second read)".
