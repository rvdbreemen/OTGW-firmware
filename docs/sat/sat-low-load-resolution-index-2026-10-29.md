It covers the review items R11, R20 and R26 to R40 of [the remediation plan](sat-low-load-remediation-plan-2026-10-28.md).

**Purpose.** A Done task or an "answered" question row says little about what is true on a boiler. This index keeps four states apart for every item:

1. **Decision state:** what Sergeant D, Robert or the specification has decided, and what is still open or only our interpretation.
2. **Implemented:** what the firmware does today, with the task and commit.
3. **Host-tested:** what a host test, a replay or a bench run without a real boiler has shown.
4. **Physically verified:** what a real boiler or heat pump has shown, on a named build.

A task that is Done, or a question that is answered, is **not** a physical-performance certificate. "Implemented" and "host-tested" never imply "physically verified". Every row below was written from source reading on 2026-10-29. Host-test results come from the task summaries and were **not re-run** in this review. No row is physically verified.

The numbered entries stay in [sergeant-d-corrections.md](sergeant-d-corrections.md) (append-only; entries 122 to 125 of 2026-10-29 are our own findings or interpretations, not Sergeant D statements). The question queue stays in [open-questions.md](open-questions.md).
