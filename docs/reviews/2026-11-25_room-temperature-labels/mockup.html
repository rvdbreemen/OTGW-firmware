Rules

  • A qualifier appears only when the two values can differ: SAT on with a source other than the thermostat.
  • SAT off, or SAT using the thermostat: plain "Room", as today.
  • One vocabulary everywhere: "room (thermostat)" and "room (SAT)"; the same words in EN, NL, DE and in the Home Assistant entity names (TASK-2973).
